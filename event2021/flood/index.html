How this forecast is made
1
Rain. Our weather model NWPLux-DA forecasts the rain for the next 48 hours, hour by hour, every 6 hours.
2
Water. A flood model (LISFLOOD-FP) lets this rain run off the land of the whole Alzette basin (467 km², 10 m detail). It starts from the river as measured now (DWD rain radar of the past hours) and from the soil moisture of the weather model.
3
Map. The highest water of the next 48 hours is laid on the 2024 laser terrain map (1 m) with all buildings from OpenStreetMap.

Blue on the map is where water outside the normal river rises during the next 48 hours: the river and its streams leaving their banks. The normal river is drawn in the lighter blue of the base map. Orange buildings have at least 30 cm of water within 3 m.

River levels are on the scale of the official AGE gauges (cm). The forecast starts from the level measured at the start and adds the change the model predicts. If the model river starts higher above normal than the gauge shows, the change is reduced by the same ratio, on the chart and on the map of that commune.

Limits. This is an experiment. In the first tests (October 2026) the model rose about 1.5 to 2 times too much in rain events, so it tends to show more flooding than will happen. Bridges, walls, pumps and pipes under streets are not in the model. Always follow the official warnings.
