How this is made
ZTD comes from E-GVAP through the GTS, read from the ECMWF archive. For each past cycle, WRFDA computes O-B (observed minus first guess, with its correction for station height) from the report nearest the cycle time. The bias of a station is its mean O-B over the last 30 days. A station needs at least 20 cycles and must lie within 300 m of the model's height at 12 km. In the assimilation, a station's bias is subtracted from its observation, the centre with the smallest spread is used per site, and sites are at least 36 km apart.
